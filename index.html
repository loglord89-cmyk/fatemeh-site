Teaching philosophy

Speak first, polish later

Fluency grows when you talk. We build the habit of speaking from the first lesson, then sharpen grammar and vocabulary on the real sentences you produce.

Know the marking criteria

IELTS rewards specific skills: task response, coherence, lexical range, grammatical accuracy and pronunciation. You will learn each criterion and practise it deliberately.

Feedback that is specific

You get clear notes on what to fix next, not a vague score. Each mistake becomes a short drill, and each drill goes into your next mock test.
